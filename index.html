The Problem

Multi-partner projects run on disconnected versions of the truth

In a joint venture, every partner maintains its own accounting records. One partner pays a supplier, another records the invoice, and a third disputes the allocation. By the time the numbers are reconciled, the project has moved on — and WIP is already out of date.

01

Separate versions of the truth

Each partner records the same economic event in a different system, at a different time, with different references and levels of detail.

02

Reconciliation happens too late

Costs are incurred continuously, but partner reconciliation and netting often happen quarterly. By then, WIP, cash exposure, and asset values are stale.

03

Evidence is disconnected

Invoices, approvals, bank proofs, and payment references sit in emails, folders, and spreadsheets. Auditing a transaction means assembling the story manually.

The problem is not that partners cannot calculate percentages. The problem is that nobody has one agreed, evidenced version of the truth.

The Solution

One verified cost record. One shared project truth.

Vericost captures every shared project cost once, attaches verifiable evidence, routes it for approval, applies contractual sharing rules, and updates WIP and partner positions in real time.

Verified cost capture

Every supplier invoice, internal recharge, variation, payroll allocation, or payment record is captured with a project code, supplier, amount, currency, invoice reference, and supporting evidence.

Partner approvals

Entitled partners approve, query, or challenge costs before they enter the agreed project-cost base. Every approval and correction is timestamped.

Automated allocation

Once approved, smart-contract logic applies the contractual sharing percentages and updates each partner’s position instantly.

Real-time WIP

Approved costs, commitments, variations, and payment statuses update WIP continuously — not at quarter-end.

Automatic netting

At period end, Vericost calculates each partner’s net position and generates settlement statements and draft invoices automatically.

Audit-grade evidence

Documents are stored securely off-chain, while cryptographic hashes are recorded on-chain to prove integrity and preserve a tamper-resistant audit trail.

Why Blockchain

Blockchain where it actually earns its place

Vericost uses a permissioned blockchain not because everything should be decentralised, but because independent partners need a shared record that none of them can quietly alter.

What goes on-chain

  • Approved cost events
  • Partner approvals
  • Document hashes
  • Payment status updates
  • Sharing-rule outcomes
  • Audit events

What stays off-chain

  • Original invoices
  • Contracts
  • Bank statements
  • Confidential commercial documents
  • Personal or sensitive data

Why this matters

  • No single partner controls the record.
  • Every approved event has a timestamped history.
  • Evidence cannot be silently altered.
  • Auditors can verify the full transaction trail.
  • Partners trust the system without trusting one counterparty’s internal database.
